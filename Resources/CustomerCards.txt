# Tycoon Mode: Chubs's custom customer cards (sold in the HQ Card Market next to the game's own cards).
#
# How to edit:
#  - Under the cards section: one line = one card:   Name | More customers % | Group size | Reputation per serve | Price | Flavour text
#      More customers %: how many more customers come each day while the card is on (30 = 30% more; at most 50).
#      Group size: what the card adds to the smallest and largest group, as smallest/largest (0/2 = groups of up to two
#        more people; 1/1 = every group one person bigger). 0/0 = no change. Groups never outgrow the biggest table.
#      Reputation per serve: what the card adds to every serve while it is on (like every other card); about 0.25 per 1%.
#      Price: what Chubs charges, in coins (about 100 + 54 x the reputation per serve: a little cheaper per reputation than Chubs's game cards, 100 + 70 x).
#    At most 20 cards are used (the first 20). Add new cards at the end (a bought card keeps its place in this list).
#  - Under the excluded section: one line = the name of a game card Chubs never sells, because it doesn't fit Tycoon.
#    TycoonChubsCards.txt (next to Player.log, written when the game starts) lists every card Chubs could sell,
#    with what it does, to help pick.
#  - Lines starting with # are notes. Save and rebuild the mod.
