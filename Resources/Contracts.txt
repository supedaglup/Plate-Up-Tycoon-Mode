# Tycoon Mode: Contracts (4-star reward, Management Desk -> Select Contracts).
#
# A contract books a special group for the NEXT day. They arrive at the marked time on the day bar. Serve every one of
# them and you get the reward; if any of them walks out (or is still waiting when the day ends) the contract fails.
# Booking takes a deposit (a quarter of the reward coins), given back on success or if the booking is cancelled.
# A failed contract also takes back every coin and all the reputation its guests earned that day.
#
# How to edit:
#  - One line = one contract:
#      Name | Arrival | Size | Patience | Reward coins | Reward reputation | Fail reputation | Fail coins | Outfit | Flavour text | Stars
#  - Arrival: when they arrive, 0 = the start of service, 1 = the end (0.5 = halfway, shown on the day bar).
#  - Size: how many people (with Take-Out on they queue one by one; every one of them must be served).
#  - Patience: their queue patience in % of a normal customer's (50 = half as patient).
#  - Reward / Fail: coins and reputation won when it succeeds, lost when it fails (fail coins are a cancellation fee).
#  - Outfit: what they wear, KitchenLib PlayerCosmeticReferences names separated by "+" (e.g. TuxChef+GlassesHat),
#    or "none". Some useful ones: TuxChef, ChefOutfit, WaiterOutfit, ApronOutfit, FirefighterOutfit, PenguinOutfit,
#    BaseballCap, CowboyHat, CrownHat, PartyConeHat, PirateHat, GlassesHat, VisorHat, TallChefHat, SantaHat, ElfHat.
#  - Stars (optional, last column): the stars a restaurant needs before it is offered (default 4). 5 = a luxury contract:
#    at 5 stars two of the day's four offers are luxury contracts.
#  - Lines starting with # are notes. At most 30 contracts are used (the first 30). The desk offers 4 random ones a day.
#  - Save and rebuild the mod. Add new contracts at the end (a booked contract keeps its place in this list).
