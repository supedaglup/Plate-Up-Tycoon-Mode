# The banker's lines (the cat in the hat at the HQ bank).
#
# How to edit:
#  - One line = one thing the banker can say. A random one is picked each time, never the same twice in a row.
#  - Lines go under a [section] heading; the section decides WHEN the line can be said (see below).
#  - [loan], [payment] and [plan] lines show in the speech bubble above the cat: keep those under 58 characters.
#    Every other section shows in the dialogue box, which has room for two or three lines of text.
#  - Lines starting with # are notes like this one and are ignored. Blank lines are ignored too.
#  - Save the file and rebuild the mod (Ctrl+Shift+B in VS Code) to use your changes.
#  - {name} in any line is replaced by the banker's name (the [name] section). His name also heads the dialogue box.
#
# Sections:
#  [name]         the banker's name (one line)
#  [introduction] the very first time the company talks to the banker (one picked)
#  [tutorial]     the bank tutorial, shown the first time someone walks into the bank's office.
#                 NOT random: each line is one page, in this order.
#  [greeting]     when a player opens the bank menu
#  [no-loan]      greeting extras when the company owes nothing (mixed in with [greeting])
#  [in-debt]      greeting extras when a loan is being repaid (mixed in with [greeting])
#  [loan]         when a loan is agreed (speech bubble)
#  [payment]      when an extra payment is made (speech bubble)
#  [plan]         when the repayment plan is changed (speech bubble)
#  [no-credit]    when the bank can't lend anything right now
#  [grace]        on the page where a new loan's or lease's plan is picked: no payment for the first {days}
#                 closings ({days} = TycoonBank.GRACE_DAYS), so the money can go into the restaurant first
#  [first-loan]   added after "I can lend you N." while the company has never borrowed: its first loan or lease
#                 comes at half the usual interest (TycoonBank.FIRST_LOAN_RATE_FACTOR)
#
# Restaurant sites (a player brings one of the blueprints from behind the banker to his desk):
#  {setting} = its location, {size} = its floor area in sqm, {price} = its price, {credit} = what the bank would lend.
#  [site-offer]     the site is for sale to this company ({setting}, {size}, {price})
#  [site-yours]     the company already bought this one
#  [site-full]      the company already has as many restaurants as it can run
#  [site-in-debt]   a loan or lease is still being paid off
#  [site-no-credit] the company's stars and reputation aren't enough for this site ({price}, {credit})
#  [site-bought]    bought outright (speech bubble, short!)
#  [site-leased]    leased (speech bubble, short!)
#  [stop-thief]     someone walks out of the bank with an unpaid blueprint; it goes back (speech bubble, short!)
#
#  [host-only]      a player who is not the host talks to the banker (only the host can do the bank's business)
