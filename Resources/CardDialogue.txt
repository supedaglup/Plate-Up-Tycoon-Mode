# Chubs's lines (the card dealer cat in the HQ Card Market, the office left of Smokey's recipe shop).
#
# How to edit (same rules as BankerDialogue.txt and ChefDialogue.txt):
#  - One line = one thing Chubs can say. A random one is picked each time, never the same twice in a row.
#  - Lines go under a [section] heading; the section decides WHEN the line can be said (see below).
#  - [bought] and [stop-thief] lines show in the speech bubble above the cat: keep those under 58 characters.
#    Every other section shows in the dialogue box, which has room for two or three lines of text.
#  - Lines starting with # are notes like this one and are ignored. Blank lines are ignored too.
#  - Save the file and rebuild the mod to use your changes.
#  - {name} in any line becomes Chubs's name (the [name] section).
#  - {card} becomes the card's name, {price} its price and {rep} the reputation it adds per customer served,
#    in the sections that mention them.
#
# Sections:
#  [name]           the dealer's name (one line)
#  [introduction]   the very first time the company talks to Chubs (one picked)
#  [tutorial]       the Card Market tutorial, shown as recipe-style popups the first time someone walks a couple of
#                   tiles into the market. NOT random: each line is one page, in this order (at most 10 pages).
#  [greeting]       when a player talks to Chubs with nothing in their paws
#  [offer]          a player brings a card from the pedestals ({card}, {price}, {rep})
#  [already-bought] a player brings a card they already paid for ({card})
#  [already-owned]  a player brings a card the company already has ({card})
#  [too-poor]       the company can't afford the card ({card}, {price})
#  [confirm]        the "are you sure?" page ({card}, {price}, {rep})
#  [bought]         a card is sold (speech bubble, short!)
#  [stop-thief]     someone walks out of the market with an unpaid card; it goes back on its pedestal (speech bubble, short!)
