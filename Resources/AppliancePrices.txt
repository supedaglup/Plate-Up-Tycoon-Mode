# Tycoon Mode appliance prices (Purchasing Desk), in coins.
#
# How to edit:
#  - One line per appliance:   Name = price
#  - The name must match the appliance's name exactly as the Purchasing Desk shows it (capital letters too).
#  - Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored. Blank lines are ignored too.
#  - An appliance not listed here gets a price from its type (see TycoonPricing.cs): ingredient crates stay cheap,
#    everything else costs more than in the normal game.
#  - Save the file and rebuild the mod (Ctrl+Shift+B in VS Code) to use your changes.
#  - Every price in use is written to TycoonPrices.txt next to Player.log, marked "list" or "formula".
#
# How the numbers were chosen (2026-11-04):
#  - Money scale: a coffee sells for about 1-2 coins (more at a table, less as take-out), so a small coffee shop
#    takes roughly 25-40 coins a day before its bill.
#  - Long-term equipment (hobs, ovens, fridges, sinks, machines) is the big investment: weeks of takings, bought
#    with a loan. Coffee is simple to serve (no plates, no washing up), so its machines cost more up front.
#  - Ingredient crates are supplies: cheap early on, but they still matter later.
#  - Upgrades cost several times their basic version; automation is for rich restaurants.
