# Smokey's lines (the chef cat in the HQ recipe shop, the old trophy room).
#
# How to edit (same rules as BankerDialogue.txt):
#  - One line = one thing Smokey can say. A random one is picked each time, never the same twice in a row.
#  - Lines go under a [section] heading; the section decides WHEN the line can be said (see below).
#  - [bought] lines show in the speech bubble above the cat: keep those under 58 characters.
#    Every other section shows in the dialogue box, which has room for two or three lines of text.
#  - Lines starting with # are notes like this one and are ignored. Blank lines are ignored too.
#  - Save the file and rebuild the mod to use your changes.
#  - {name} in any line becomes Smokey's name (the [name] section).
#  - {recipe} becomes the recipe's name and {price} its price, in the sections that mention them.
#
# Sections:
#  [name]           the chef's name (one line)
#  [introduction]   the very first time the company talks to Smokey (one picked)
#  [tutorial]       the recipe shop tutorial, shown as recipe-style popups the first time someone walks a couple of
#                   tiles into the shop. NOT random: each line is one page, in this order (at most 10 pages).
#  [greeting]       when a player talks to Smokey with nothing in their paws
#  [offer]          a player brings a recipe from the shelves ({recipe}, {price})
#  [already-bought] a player brings a recipe they already paid for ({recipe})
#  [already-owned]  a player brings a recipe their restaurant already knows ({recipe})
#  [too-poor]       the company can't afford the recipe ({recipe}, {price})
#  [confirm]        the "are you sure?" page ({recipe}, {price})
#  [bought]         a recipe is sold (speech bubble, short!)
#  [stop-thief]     someone walks out of the shop with an unpaid recipe; it goes back on its shelf (speech bubble, short!)
#  [host-only]      a player who is not the host talks to Smokey (only the host can buy recipes)
